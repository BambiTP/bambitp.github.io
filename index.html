Clones

Seven attempts at rebuilding TagPro from scratch — Box2D physics, PIXI rendering, and a bit more of the real game each time. In order.

  1. February 2025
    Tagpro Next

    The first one. A playable single-player client with Box2D physics, PIXI rendering, textures, sounds and a bot to play against.

    92 commits · Play · Source
  2. April 2025
    Blitz Arena

    A sandbox for the parts of the game that aren't the ball: gravity wells, portals and speedpads, each with its own tile art.

    7 commits · Play · Source
  3. May – June 2026
    Tagpro Open

    The rewrite that stuck. Split into real modules — map loader, contact listener, physics data, renderer, replay player — and became the base every later engine was cut from.

    16 commits · Play · Source
  4. June 2026
    Replay Viewer

    The engine pointed at recorded games instead of live ones: decodes the packet stream and replays a match through the same physics.

    5 commits · Open · Source
  5. July – August 2026
    GrabTag private

    Where it turned multiplayer. A Node server with rooms, accounts, reconnect handling and a leader who controls match settings, physics and the map — edited live, mid-game.

    23 commits
  6. August 2026
    BamBall

    The furthest it has gone. A proper engine module set, two extra game modes — Gravity and Eggball — plus a TagPro-style scoreboard, chat channels, flair, and a Cloudflare Worker behind it.

    61 commits · Play · Source
  7. August 2026
    Live Map Editor

    The engine turned into a tool: paint tiles and swap texture packs while the game keeps running around you.

    12 commits · Open · Source

Ranked Replay Archive

A census of every ranked match ever played — 99.99% of the known ids — and a growing store of the recordings themselves. Refreshed daily. Records that were never published are rebuilt here from the raw recording.

NALTP Archive

The full history of the North American leagues — MLTP, mLTP, NLTP and ALTP — with schedules, drafts, rosters and trades. A fact counts as confirmed only when two separate sources agree; everywhere they disagree is published too.
